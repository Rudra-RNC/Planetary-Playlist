# NASA EIC Data Catalog

The current six-track playlist uses still frames from NASA Scientific Visualization Studio pages in six EIC themes. The local files are compressed copies of the cited NASA preview frames; the app maps visible pixels, not raw measurements.

| Track | EIC theme | NASA source | Local asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tidal Memory | Sea level change | [Sea Surface Height Anomaly](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5504/) | `public/tracks/tide.webp` |
| Canopy Lift | Biodiversity | [GEDI Forest Height](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4950/) | `public/tracks/canopy.webp` |
| Atmospheric Drift | Air quality | [Air Quality Model Runs](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5533/) | `public/tracks/air.webp` |
| Carbon Weather | Greenhouse gases | [DYAMOND Global Carbon Dioxide](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5196/) | `public/tracks/carbon.webp` |
| Afterglow | Energy | [Change in Night Lights](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5313/) | `public/tracks/lights.webp` |
| Fireline | Wildfires | [Active Fires Observed by VIIRS](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5113/) | `public/tracks/fire.webp` |

The official [EIC gallery](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/earth-information-center/) also exposes future playlist candidates for water resources, agriculture, disasters, ocean currents, and daily precipitation. Those are intentionally deferred until the six-track P0 playlist is judged stable.
